HOW PAY IN 4 WORKS

More Freedom Without Missing What Matters.

Unexpected expenses can put pressure on any budget, but paying an essential bill shouldn't mean compromising everything else. Splitano's Pay in 4 service is designed for eligible utility, insurance and medical bills, giving you a more flexible way to manage important payments. Once your application is approved, Splitano pays your bill directly to your provider, allowing you to repay the balance through four scheduled payments. Instead of facing one large payment at once, you gain a structured plan that helps you stay on track while keeping your finances more manageable.

WHY CHOOSE PAY IN 4

Built Around Flexibility. Backed By Transparency.

Choosing Splitano means choosing a payment experience designed with clarity from the very beginning. There are no hidden fees or unexpected charges after approval and every payment is clearly outlined before you commit, giving you complete confidence in your repayment schedule. The service isn't limited to just one bill either, you can use Splitano for multiple eligible utility, insurance, and medical bills over time.
